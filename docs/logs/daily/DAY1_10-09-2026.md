## Key decisions

- **`numpy<2` pinned.** Python is 3.11.16, so current versions were taken for
  everything else; numpy is pinned because `neurokit2==0.2.7` predates the ABI
  break. A consequence of the neurokit2 pin, not of the Python version.
- **Grad-CAM hand-rolled**, not the `grad-cam` package — ~40 lines of hooks, and
  inlining it keeps the script runnable inside a Kaggle kernel.
- **`04_segment_qc` writes a new directory** instead of repairing `03`'s output
  in place. The first version mutated its own dependency, which DVC cannot track
  and which destroyed the evidence of what had been repaired.
- **Augmentation seeds per (segment, variant)**, extending the brief's
  per-segment example, so adding or reordering variants cannot silently change
  existing values.
- **`method="fft"` and PIL's antialiased resize** in the scalogram stage. The
  first is 4.2× faster and numerically identical; the second is a *correctness*
  fix — `scipy.ndimage.zoom` point-samples and loses 3.1% of the field's mean at
  a 27× time-axis reduction.
- **Reflect-pad before the CWT** (see Blockers).
- **Training goes to Kaggle.** Measured ~500 s/epoch on CPU vs ~27 s/epoch on a
  T4. Local 5-fold CV across all families would be ~120 h; it does not finish.
- **`scripts/remote/05_run_queue.py` added** beyond the brief's 01–04, because
  Kaggle caps batch GPU sessions at 2 and ~60 jobs turn "push, poll, fetch" into
  a scheduler. It also keeps the quota ledger §9 requires.

## Blockers

**The CWT boundary artifact — resolved, at the cost of discarded work.**

Rendering the first paper figure showed the ECG scalogram was almost entirely
dark: two bright bands at the edges, near-black between. Outer columns carried
~19× the interior energy; interior mean 4.6/255; 85% of pixels below 26/255.
PCG was fine.

Cause: the CWT cone of influence at ECG scales up to 500, where the wavelet is
as long as the 6000-sample window. Fixed by reflect-padding by `4 × max(scale)`
and cropping back — interior mean 4.6 → 59.3, edge/interior 19.29 → 0.55.

Stopped the training queue, discarded 4 completed kernels and 3 scalogram
configs, and regenerated. **This was worth doing rather than noting**: Grad-CAM
would have highlighted the artifact and Contribution 2 would have reported a
false physiological conclusion.

Every numeric QC gate passed while this was wrong. It took looking at a picture.

## Deviations from the brief, and why

1. **Deleted `training-b` … `training-f` and `validation`** with the rest of
   `DATASET/`. §1.2's Keep list names only `training-a`, the other subsets are
   PCG-only and cannot serve a dual-modality project, and the owner holds a full
   copy elsewhere. Flagged because it goes slightly beyond the literal list.
2. **`dvc.yaml` created in Step 3, not Step 2.** Stage definitions pointing at
   scripts that do not exist yet cannot be validated. `dvc init` was done in
   Step 2; stages were added alongside the scripts they run.
3. **The brief's ECG frequency annotation is corrected, not followed.** Scales
   `arange(20, 501)` with `cmor1.5-1.0` at 2000 Hz give **4.0–100.0 Hz**, not
   the stated "≈0.5–40 Hz" — verified by running `pywt.cwt`. The *scales* are
   kept exactly as specified, since they are the explicit part of the spec and
   §10.1 requires them fixed across wavelets. **The measured numbers are what
   should go in the paper.**
4. **`scripts/remote/05_run_queue.py` added** beyond the specified 01–04, for
   the concurrency cap and the quota ledger.
5. **Reflect-padding added to the scalogram transform.** The brief specifies the
   wavelet, scales, `np.abs`, and the resize; it does not specify boundary
   handling. Padding is a fix to an artifact, not a change to the specified
   transform.
